These photographs show a selection of Geli Construction Services coloured concrete work across several residential properties. The gallery includes curved driveway layouts, a compact driveway framed by garden beds, and a dark concrete entry area.

Charcoal and other coloured concrete finishes can give driveways, paths and entries a defined appearance while retaining the practical function of a concrete surface.
