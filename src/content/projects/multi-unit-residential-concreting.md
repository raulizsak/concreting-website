This showcase brings together genuine examples of Geli Construction Services concreting work from more than one multi-unit residential development. The photographs document reinforcement and formwork preparation, concrete pumping and placement, and completed common access areas.

The finished work includes both plain and dark-coloured concrete surfaces shaped around the buildings, drainage points and shared routes through the developments.
