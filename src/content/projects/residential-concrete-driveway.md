This gallery brings together genuine examples of Geli Construction Services residential driveway work from several properties. It includes an original project sequence from reinforcement preparation through to a completed plain concrete driveway, together with additional finished driveways, street crossovers and a connecting concrete path.

The different views show how plain concrete can form practical vehicle and pedestrian access around new and existing residential construction.
